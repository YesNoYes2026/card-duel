How to play
  1. Goal: wipe out your opponent’s characters.
  2. Hit: a character plus an action. Add a special if your character has its speciality; if not, it counts as +1.
  3. Clash: the attacker hits and the defender answers. Match or beat it and you’re safe. Fall short and you sacrifice characters whose Strength covers the gap.
  4. Boost: block by more than you need, or sacrifice more Strength than the gap, and the extra fills your Boost ring. Spend 1 Boost to Charge a character (+2, up to twice), attack twice, or replace a character.
  5. Refresh: each turn, replace one action and one special for free.
